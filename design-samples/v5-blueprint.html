A-01 / Site surveyExisting conditions

The fault is never where the crack shows.

A clinic loses bookings and concludes it needs more marketing. In fact the front desk is retyping the same patient into three places, and the enquiry that arrived at nine at night was never seen at all.

Survey your own site before reading further. Count the enquiries that arrived after 9pm last month, then count how many got an answer before 10am. That difference is not a marketing number — it is structural. Advertising will not move it; it sends more people down the same corridor.

We have yet to open an engagement where the presenting complaint turned out to be the actual constraint.

A-02 / Sequence of worksOut of order = rework

You cannot automate a process nobody has defined.

Automation applied to an undefined process does not save time. It makes the mess arrive faster, and with more confidence. So it is scheduled last — always.

PH-01

StrategizeSurvey & setting out

We map how work really moves — where enquiries arrive, where they stall, and which step quietly costs the most. Most businesses do not have a growth problem. They have a structure problem, usually three steps upstream of the pain.

PH-02

SystemizeStructure & frame

Scattered tools are the tax. The spreadsheet-and-inbox arrangement is replaced by a single spine: one place enquiries land, one calendar with real availability, one record per client that every screen reads from.

PH-03

AutomateServices & fit-out

Only now does routine work run itself — follow-ups that fire without being remembered, notifications that reach the right person, an assistant that answers at 2am in the customer's own language, Bangla included.

A-06 / Maintenance & revisionsNo naked builds

A launch is the worst the system will ever be.

How the partnership works →

On the day it goes live, a system has no data. Nobody knows yet which step loses people, which message gets ignored, or which report the owner actually opens. Everything useful is learned afterwards.

Which is why most agencies are structured badly for you. They are paid to leave — issue the drawings, invoice, move on. Nobody returns to see whether the building works in the rain.

We would rather be paid to stay. Every build is issued with a care plan, and revisions are the point rather than an admission.
