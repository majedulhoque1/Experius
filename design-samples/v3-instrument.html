01 / Diagnosis The fault is upstream of the alarm

The leak is never where the alarm is.

A clinic loses bookings and concludes it needs more marketing. In fact the front desk is retyping the same patient into three places, and the enquiry that arrived at nine at night was never seen at all.

Run the check yourself. Count the enquiries that arrived after 9pm last month. Now count how many got an answer before 10am. That difference is not a marketing number — it is structural, and ad spend will not move it. It sends more people down the same broken corridor.

We have yet to open an engagement where the presenting complaint turned out to be the actual constraint.

02 / System Six modules · one record · one bus

Everything writes to the same record.

This is the whole difference. Not six tools that integrate — six modules that were never separate. The booking form writes the record the dashboard reads from, and the automation fires off that same record. Nothing is stitched together after the fact, because nothing was ever apart.

05 / Uptime No naked builds

A launch is the worst the system will ever be.

How the partnership works →

On the day it goes live, a system has no data. Nobody knows yet which step loses people, which message gets ignored, or which report the owner actually opens. Everything useful is learned afterwards.

Which is why most agencies are structured badly for you. They are paid to leave — ship something that looks finished, invoice, move on. Eighteen months later nobody has checked whether it worked.

We would rather be paid to stay. Every build ships with a care plan attached, and the months after launch are when it earns back what it cost.
