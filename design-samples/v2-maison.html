On the nature
of the problem

The leak is almost never where it hurts.

A clinic loses bookings and concludes it needs more marketing. In fact the front desk is retyping the same patient into three separate places, and the enquiry that arrived at nine at night was never seen at all. The money is not lost at the point of pain. It is lost three steps upstream, quietly, every day, in a place nobody has thought to look.

Do the arithmetic on your own business before you read any further. Count the enquiries that arrived after nine o'clock last month. Now count how many received an answer before ten the next morning. That gap is not a marketing number. No amount of advertising will move it, because advertising sends more people into the same broken corridor.

We have yet to open an engagement where the presenting complaint turned out to be the actual constraint. Not once.

The method

Three movements, and the order is the argument.

Automation applied to an undefined process does not save time. It makes the mess arrive faster, and with more confidence. So it comes last, always.

I

StrategizeFind where it leaks

We map how the work truly moves — where enquiries arrive, where they stall, and which single step quietly costs the most. Most businesses do not have a growth problem. They have a structure problem wearing a growth problem's clothes.

II

SystemizeOne place for the work

Scattered tools are the tax. The spreadsheet-and-inbox arrangement is replaced by a single spine: one place enquiries land, one calendar with real availability, one record per client that every screen reads from.

III

AutomateOff your desk entirely

Only now does the routine work begin to run itself. Follow-ups that fire without being remembered. Notifications that reach the right person. An assistant that answers at two in the morning, in Bangla if that is what the customer speaks.

Notes on the figures

On staying

A launch is the worst the system will ever be.

On the day it goes live, a system has no data. Nobody yet knows which step loses people, which message is ignored, or which report the owner actually opens. Everything useful is learned afterwards — which is precisely when most agencies leave, because they are paid to. We would rather be paid to stay. No naked builds: every system ships with a care plan attached.
