Why these two and not a list of industries
A clinic and a children's charity look nothing alike from the outside. From the inside they fail identically: the most valuable contact either will ever receive — a new patient, a worried parent — arrives into an inbox at an hour when nobody is watching, and is answered by whoever happens to look. Everything we build is aimed at that moment. Other work we take because the shape is the same.
